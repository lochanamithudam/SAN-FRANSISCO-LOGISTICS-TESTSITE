2. Core Automation Features

Modern automated gantry cranes eliminate high-altitude manual cabs, replacing them with integrated artificial intelligence, sub-millimeter LiDAR sensors, and automated Terminal Operating System scheduling.

Remote Operation Stations (ROS)

Crane drivers work from a temperature-controlled, ergonomic office control room using curved 4K multi-screen monitors, HD camera feeds, and precision joysticks instead of sitting 150 ft in the air.

  • Eliminates physical vibration, neck strain, and cab climbing hazards.
  • Seamless operator handoffs between quay STS and yard ARMG cranes.
  • Low-latency video compression ($<20\text{ ms}$) over dual fiber.

OCR & Smart Sensors

Integrated high-speed cameras on the spreader scan container numbers, ISO codes, hazardous placards, seal integrity, and structural damage in real time upon container pickup.

  • 99.8% Optical Character Recognition accuracy during high-speed hoist.
  • Instant seal integrity verification and damage photographic record.
  • Automated customs API validation and weight tare verification.

Positioning & 3D LiDAR Anti-Sway

Laser scanners (LiDAR), 3D spatial vision, and predictive electronic anti-sway algorithms calculate hoist inertia and wind load to achieve millimetric container landing without manual swaying.

  • Millimeter-accurate spreader twistlock alignment in 25 knot crosswinds.
  • Continuous 3D point cloud profiling of vessel cell guides and yard stacks.
  • Dynamic collision avoidance matrix preventing spreader-vessel contact.

Terminal Operating System (TOS) Integration

Direct real-time API sync with industry-standard TOS engines like Navis N4, CyberLogitec, or Tops to automatically route moves, manage vessel stowage, and optimize yard stacking order.

  • Autonomous work instruction dispatch directly to crane PLC controllers.
  • Predictive vessel discharge sequencing minimizing idle crane cycle time.
  • Real-time inventory sync across gate, rail, and marine berths.

Site Engineering

3. Infrastructure Requirements for a San Francisco Site

Integrating 4 automated gantry cranes at San Francisco maritime berths (Pier 80, Pier 94/96, or inland intermodal yards) requires strict civil foundation upgrades, zero-emission CARB/BAAQMD electrical grid connections, and Bay Bridge air draft navigation clearance.

01

Civil & Structural Foundation

Pier 80 and Pier 94/96 maritime berths feature wharf design loads ranging from 800 to 1,000 lbs/sq.ft. New automated cranes place massive dynamic loads during high-speed acceleration and container landings.

Wharf Bearing Load
Super Post-Panamax cranes weigh 1,350 to 1,650 metric tons each. Wheel point loads exceed 60–80 tonnes/meter; piling dynamic analysis required.
Rail Gauge & Alignment
Precision gauge tolerances ($\pm 2\text{ mm}$ over 300+ m) utilizing heavy-duty A100 or A120 crane rail sections with high-durability epoxy grout retrofits.
SF-Oakland Bay Bridge Air Draft
180–220 ft air draft clearance under Bay Bridge. Large cranes must be shipped on heavy-lift vessels with booms lowered and erected on-site.
02

Electrical & Zero-Emission Grid

California Air Resources Board (CARB) and the Bay Area Air Quality Management District (BAAQMD) mandate zero-emission port operations. All 4 cranes operate 100% on electric grid power.

100% Electric Shore Power
Strict zero-emission compliance eliminates all diesel emissions, particulate matter, and NOx across the waterfront facility.
High-Voltage Substation
Dedicated on-site step-down substation ($12\text{kV}-25\text{kV} \rightarrow 4,160\text{V} \text{ and } 480\text{V}$) to supply continuous peak hoisting and gantry acceleration.
Cable Reels & Regenerative Braking
Heavy-duty motorized cable reels and regen units that feed kinetic energy back into the local grid during container lowering, cutting power costs by ~35%.
03

Communication & Safety Setup

Autonomous gantry movements require deterministic, low-latency data communication and physical zone interlocks to protect personnel and equipment.

Private 5G & Dedicated Fiber
Ultra-low latency ($< 20\text{ ms}$) wireless and fiber network delivering 4K live video feeds and bi-directional telemetry to the ROS and TOS.
Optical Safety Fencing & Interlocks
Automated crane zones isolated with optical light curtains, perimeter radar/laser scanners, and automated gate e-stops that freeze crane travel upon entry.
Cybersecurity & Fail-Safe PLC
IEC 62443 industrial security compliant with dual-redundant safety PLCs executing automatic emergency stop routines upon link loss.
